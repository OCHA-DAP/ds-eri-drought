How Eritrea's 2026 rainy season is going

The June to August rains were among the poorest Eritrea has had since records began in 1981. Vegetation is falling fast after a good start to the year, and the dry area grew through the season.

Rainfall

This season is one of the driest on record. Every region got well below its normal rainfall from June to August. Since 1981, only 1984 and 1990 were drier across the country, and 1991 was about as dry. This isn't a patchy or local shortfall: the whole country missed out on its main rains.

Vegetation

Above-normal rains from March to May boosted vegetation early in the year, but the main June to August rains, which bring most of the year's rainfall, failed.

Vegetation peaked in early May and has fallen steadily since June. Some areas dropped below normal in late July, and more in late August. Other areas are still above normal but falling. The early-season greenness is running out, and the steady downward trend suggests conditions will keep getting worse over the coming weeks.

Drought indicator

ICPAC's drought indicator shows the dry conditions spreading steadily from June to August. They started in the south and by August covered most of the south and centre of the country, and a strip along the northern coast.

So far the indicator only shows the lack of rain. It hasn't yet picked up effects on soil moisture or vegetation, which follow a rainfall shortage.
